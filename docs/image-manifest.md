# Image and font manifest

The redesigned website uses actual licensed photographs, served locally. No AI-generated destination or customer photograph is used in the active design. Sources were retrieved from Wikimedia Commons during the build; metadata is maintained in `src/data/media.json`.

Images are resized to responsive WebP variants and cropped in page layouts; the Thailand hero also has portrait mobile derivatives. Derivatives retain their source license. Original credit links and authors appear on the site credits page; gallery images also link directly to their source.
