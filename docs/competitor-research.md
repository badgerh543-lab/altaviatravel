# Travel UX research — 18 November 2026

Public-page research informed the redesign. No competitor code, branding, distinctive layout or photographs were copied. Text inspection establishes the observations below; visual judgments are labeled as design interpretations. No bookings or inquiries were submitted.

## Coverage and limits

For all 11 accessible sources, homepage text hierarchy, hero/headline role, navigation, discovery entry points, visual-storytelling role, trust claims and CTA labels were considered from available public pages. Destination/tour detail inspection was deepest on Advantour, Anur and Intrepid; the other sources' deeper detail/checkout flows were not fully exercised. Inquiry/booking observations concern visible entry points only, never successful delivery.

Mobile responsiveness, keyboard behavior, actual color contrast and performance were **not measured across competitor sites**. Raw page extraction cannot prove these characteristics. No competitor Lighthouse scores or accessibility claims are invented here. SEO observations are limited to visible headings, linked destination/detail structure and descriptive content; no competitor canonical/schema audit was completed. These are research limits, not favorable assumptions.

## Decisions for ALTAVIA

1. A large photographic hero communicates both Uzbekistan and international travel; the adjacent search takes visitors directly into a shareable catalog.
2. Country, destination, type, duration, style, season and budget affordances accompany clear route/day information. Unpriced route ideas remain explicit quote requests.
3. Each destination has original local-language editorial content, highlights and relevant itineraries. Daily itinerary detail gives substance beyond a photo card.
4. The custom planner narrows preferences in three steps and shows a review before inquiry. Success depends on actual saved data.
5. Honest process statements replace unverifiable ratings, awards, customer photos and supplier promises. Licensed editorial images carry individual credits.
6. Typography and composition provide distinction: Unbounded/Manrope, sun-yellow and vivid orange, destination circles, asymmetric imagery, and varied section rhythm.
7. ALTAVIA's own mobile, accessibility, security and performance behavior is tested separately and recorded in `qa-report.md`; competitor reputation is not used as evidence for our implementation.
