Every initial catalog value is `market-estimate`, displayed as an estimated USD amount per person. These are budget guides, not confirmed Altavia offers, availability or contracted hotel rates. Final dates, passenger count, sharing basis, transport and inclusions require a written quote. Hotel examples remain independent of the price. No comparison is presented as an identical package.

The USD 155/day proxy is calculated from a regular eight-day USD 1,240 group comparison at [Advantour](https://www.advantour.com/uzbekistan/tours.htm). It is an editorial estimate for the shorter routes, not an advertised rate from that source. The mountain example is [Advantour's two-day Gulkam/Small Chimgan program](https://www.advantour.com/uzbekistan/tours/gulkam-small-chimgan-tour.htm).

[SEM Travel's catalog](https://semtravel.uz/ru/tours) supplied the Istanbul and flight/hotel package comparisons. Its page presents both search starting rates and individual package amounts, sometimes with differing durations; the seed uses package amounts where available, rather than implying that the lowest search rate covers Altavia's itinerary. Cappadocia uses the operator's published [2026 four-day comparison](https://www.sojournturkeytours.com/tours/cappadocia-77992). Malaysia uses [1Travel's published package starting point](https://1travel.uz/malajziya/).

Each tour stores its source, check date and basis for staff review. Change to `verified-from` only after Altavia approves a real saleable starting price and its exact inclusions. New/edited tours require a positive price and image in the panel. Staff should refresh these estimates regularly and before quoting; public source pages and currencies can change. The one-time seed script preserves existing prices and should not be used to overwrite CMS content.
